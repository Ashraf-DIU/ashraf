About Me

I am a research-oriented AI/ML Engineer with extensive experience in Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Explainable AI (XAI), and Medical Image Analysis. My primary research focus revolves around building trustworthy, interpretable, and practical AI systems that solve high-stakes challenges.

Currently serving as a Research Assistant at the DIU ML and NLP Research Lab, I contribute to end-to-end research pipelines—from multi-slice dataset curation and architectural prototyping to rigorous model evaluation and manuscript publication.

While my foundational work targets visual representation learning and interpretable medical decision-making, I am actively expanding into Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Multimodal AI systems — building full-stack AI applications along the way.
